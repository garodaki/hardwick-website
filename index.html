After a storm

How an insurance roof job works with us

You own your claim. We do the roofing and give you clear documentation so you and your insurance company have what you need.

Free inspection

We get on the roof, photograph any damage and tell you honestly whether it is worth filing a claim.

You file the claim

If there is real damage, you call your insurance company. We give you our photo report to share.

Adjuster visit

We can be on site when your adjuster inspects, to point out the damage we documented.

We build

Once your claim is approved, we schedule the job, install the new roof and clean up.

Straight talk on deductibles. Texas law requires homeowners to pay their insurance deductible, and it is illegal for a contractor to waive or cover it. We never do. If a roofer offers to, walk away. We also do not act as your public adjuster; we are your roofer.
