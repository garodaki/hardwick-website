FAQ

Roofing questions, answered

How much does a roof replacement cost in Flower Mound and DFW?

Most asphalt shingle roof replacements on DFW homes land somewhere around $8,000 to $20,000, depending on roof size, pitch, layers to remove and material. Impact-rated shingles and metal cost more. We give a written, itemized price after a free on-site measurement.

Do you help with hail damage insurance claims?

Yes. We inspect for free, document damage with photos and can meet your adjuster on site. You own and file your claim. Texas law requires homeowners to pay their deductible, and we never waive or cover it.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family roofs are replaced in one to two days, weather permitting. Larger or steeper roofs can take longer. We clean up and run a magnetic nail sweep before we leave.

Are you licensed?

Texas does not issue a state license for roofing or general contracting. We pull city permits where required, carry insurance, and use state-licensed partners for plumbing, electrical and HVAC work.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Flower Mound and serve Denton County and the DFW Metroplex, including Highland Village, Lewisville, Argyle, Lantana, Denton, Southlake, Grapevine, Coppell, Carrollton, The Colony, Frisco, Keller, Trophy Club and Roanoke.

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. The inspection and written estimate are free with no obligation. If your roof does not need work, we will tell you.
