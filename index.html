02 / After a storm

Four steps from hail to a new roof.

You own your claim. We do the roofing and hand you clear photo documentation, so you and your insurance company have what you need.

STEP 1

Free inspection

We get on the roof, photograph any damage and tell you straight whether a claim makes sense.

STEP 2

You file the claim

If the damage is real, you call your insurance company and share our photo report.

STEP 3

Adjuster visit

We can be there when your adjuster inspects, to point out the damage we documented.

STEP 4

We build

Once your claim is approved, we schedule, install the new roof and clean up the same day.

Straight talk on deductibles. Texas law requires homeowners to pay their insurance deductible, and it is illegal for a contractor to waive or cover it. We never do. If a roofer offers to, walk away. We are your roofer, not your public adjuster.
